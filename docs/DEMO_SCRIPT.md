# Five-minute demonstration

## 0:00 — A commander, not a quiz

Open the game start screen. Say: “ASTRA puts children in charge of one lunar outpost. Their choices change their supplies, future power, and final mission outcome.” Meet Captain Nova, click an illustrated outpost hotspot, then select **Start your mission** to enter the briefing directly.

## 0:30 — Prepare a plan

Choose Pathfinder in the briefing and accept it. Explain that no real name or account is needed. Optional crew onboarding remains under Missions. Keep the default **Extra oxygen + Repair toolkit** loadout. Secure it and begin the lunar landing from the launch cabin.

## 1:15 — A problem with consequences

Inspect the habitat. The broken solar connector is revealed. Show the decision desk: repairing consumes supplies now but restores power for future turns; continuing on battery preserves immediate supplies but sacrifices generation. Return to exploration. Select **Solar array**, travel, and repair. Watch the real resource changes and ORBIT's explanation.

## 2:00 — Explore and respond

Select **Sunlit Ridge**, travel, and collect the rock sample. Select **Shackleton Edge** and travel. Point out the scripted particle alert and the fact that the risk display uses educational points, not a real radiation dose. Explain that the habitat or field shelter can protect time spent waiting, but travel there still uses supplies. Collect the soil sample. For the short demonstration, return directly while reserves remain sufficient.

## 3:00 — Bring the crew home

Travel to **Artemis Base** and complete the mission. Show the excellent outcome, reserves, sample count, and achievement labels. Open the learning report. Show decision → resource delta → explanation in the timeline, and export the journal.

## 3:45 — Show why the science is trustworthy

Open Science Explorer. Expand an official NASA reference: source, retrieval date, observation date if supplied, use in the game, and limitations. Show that historical media is labeled as historical. ORBIT topic buttons work without an AI key. Avoid saying the map, events, or resource values come from live NASA data.

## 4:15 — Replay changes the mission

Describe or start the battery expedition: extra oxygen + rover, select battery power, collect both samples, return. It produces science-complete rather than excellent because the array remains broken. A short one-sample excursion produces a safe return with reduced science. Repeated outside waiting can exhaust a safety reserve or mission time, ending a practice mission with a learning report.

## 4:45 — Inclusion and implementation

Show pause/resume and settings for reduced motion, larger text, and optional guidance. Explain the server-authoritative deterministic engine, anonymous persistence, retry protection, and automated test coverage. The original map remains available without NASA enrichment. End on the idea: “Every choice shapes your mission.”
